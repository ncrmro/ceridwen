# Snap-together enclosure

The current enclosure uses five printed pieces: base, captured electronics tray,
lid with integral component clips, and two snap-in button caps. It has no loose
fasteners, separate mounting deck or stop plates. This replaces the 32-piece
push-pin design.

The default body is 88 × 61 × 38.25 mm. Component measurements, integral snaps,
cap flexures and clearances remain editable. Battery retention has clearance
and must not compress the pouch.

See [the parametric design guide](parametric-design.md) for the part-count
comparison, assembly/removal sequence, parameters, exports and verification
limits. Seated solid checks and button travel pass; snap insertion force,
material fatigue and physical assembly still require a trial print.
